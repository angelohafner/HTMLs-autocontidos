Fundamental amplitude: Vpeak=vd2+vq2. The angle is φ=atan2(vq,vd). A nonzero v0 adds the same DC offset to every phase: this changes phase-to-neutral waveforms but does not affect their fundamental phasors or phase-to-phase voltages.
